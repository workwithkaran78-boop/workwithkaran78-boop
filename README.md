## About Me

I started in mechanical engineering: AutoCAD drawings, manufacturing, and quality-control workflows. That work taught me how real processes break down and how much time repetitive tasks waste.

Today I run **Xtoa AI**, an AI automation studio in Ahmedabad, India, for small and mid-sized businesses such as hotels, gyms, educational institutions, and B2B manufacturers. I figure out what a business needs, then direct AI tools to build it: websites, WhatsApp chatbots, and workflow automations.

I'm a founder and builder, not a traditional software engineer. I focus on practical solutions to real business problems, and I'm learning something new with every project.

## My Approach

1. **Understand the business problem.** Start with the repetitive work, not the tool.
2. **Design a practical solution.** The simplest system that actually solves it.
3. **Build and integrate.** Connect the chatbot, data, and apps into one flow.
4. **Test and improve.** Run it on real scenarios and fix what breaks.
5. **Measure the value.** Time saved and enquiries handled, not vanity numbers.
